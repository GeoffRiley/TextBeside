# Synthetic field notes

Two paired image and Markdown files for trying TextBeside's core workflow:

- `field-notes-01.jpg` ↔ `field-notes-01.md`
- `field-notes-02.jpg` ↔ `field-notes-02.md`

The images and words are fictitious. These pages were drawn programmatically for TextBeside in September 2026; they do not depict a historical source, contain personal data, or depend on a third-party image. They are included under the repository's MIT licence.

Open this folder as a collection when directory navigation is available. Until then, open an image and its matching text file manually. The two portrait pages exercise page navigation, zoom, pan, fit-to-image, and text persistence. Try editing a transcription and switching pages to check that work is saved or guarded against loss.

These are simple workflow fixtures, not an OCR benchmark or a realistic test of difficult handwriting. Add a separately attributed and redistributable handwriting sample later if that becomes important.
